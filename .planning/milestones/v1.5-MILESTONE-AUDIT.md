## Executive Summary

Milestone v1.5 substantively delivers what was planned: a working `install.sh` + `install.ps1` (Claude + Copilot branches), `README.md` with Quick Start one-liners and Mermaid diagram, MIT `LICENSE`, and `AGENTS.md` for cross-tool support. Manual artifact inspection confirms 14/15 ROADMAP success criteria are met (1 needs human verification in VS Code).

However, the audit returns **gaps_found** because:

1. **Two phases (19, 20) have no VERIFICATION.md and no SUMMARY.md.** Per the GSD workflow, missing VERIFICATION.md → unverified phase → blocker. Plans are checked `[x]` in ROADMAP and artifacts exist, but the GSD verification step was skipped during execution.
2. **REQUIREMENTS.md is stale** — INST-07, INST-08, COP-03 text still describes the original design that was explicitly superseded by D-01, D-02, D-03 locked in 21-CONTEXT.md before implementation. The ROADMAP was updated; REQUIREMENTS.md was not.
3. **Two intentional divergences are undocumented in REQUIREMENTS.md:** DOC-05 (GitHub Sponsors button instead of PayPal/Venmo links) and INST-06 (D-06: always-overwrite CLAUDE.md instead of skip-if-present).
4. **Phase 21 VERIFICATION.md is stale** — written 2026-04-22, then commits 2fa0413 and 4e90677 added material logic (workflows + templates installation in copilot branch) that VERIFICATION.md does not cover.

The substance is shipped. The bookkeeping is incomplete.
